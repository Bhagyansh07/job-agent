# Writing style (cover letters, "why us" fields, founder notes, LinkedIn DMs)

Purpose: everything the agent writes must sound like a real person wrote it, not AI.

## Dashes
- Never use em dash or en dash. Use a comma, a period, or a plain hyphen "-".

## Banned words and phrases (do not use these, ever)
- "I am excited to"
- "passionate about"
- "leverage"
- "dynamic"
- "hit the ground running"
- "I came across your posting"
- "esteemed organisation"
- "team player"
- "detail-oriented"
- "I would be a great fit"
- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "delve"
- "seamless"
- "robust"
- "cutting-edge"

## Rhythm and tone
- No emoji in cover letters. No stacked exclamation marks. Do not force three-item lists everywhere (the classic AI rhythm).
- Vary sentence length; mix short and medium lines. Write like you are talking to a person.

## Facts
- Only facts from resume/master.json. Never invent a skill, a number or a project.
- Name the company's actual product and one of Bhagyansh's real projects that matches it.

## Length
- Cover letter: 3 to 5 lines.
- Founder note: 2 to 3 lines.
- LinkedIn note: under 280 characters.

## Read it back
- If a sentence could appear in any generic application, rewrite it.

## Pre-submit checklist (run this in your head before writing any text)
1. Any em dash or en dash? Replace with comma, period or hyphen.
2. Any banned word or phrase above? Remove or rewrite.
3. Does it name the company's real product and one real project from master.json?
4. Are all facts in master.json (no invented skill, number or project)?
5. Length within limits (cover letter 3-5 lines, founder note 2-3, LinkedIn note under 280 chars)?
6. No emoji, no stacked exclamation marks, no three-item list filler?
7. Does every sentence sound like a specific human, not a template? If not, rewrite.
